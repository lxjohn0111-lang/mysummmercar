# Rustbucket Summer

## Short description (one line)
Build a rusty old car from a hundred parts, survive a crazy Finnish summer, drive, race and party in the countryside.

## Description
Summer 1997, Kurkijärvi, Finland. Your uncle has left for the oil rigs and left you his lakeside house, a tired old van, a moped, a tractor – and a Ruska 1300 lying in a hundred pieces in the garage.

Pick up every part with your own hands, fit it on the car and tighten the bolts with your wrench: suspension, brakes, wheels, the whole engine, wiring, exhaust and seats. Charge the battery, pour in the oil, coolant and fuel, turn the key and hear it come alive for the first time. Pass the vehicle inspection, then tune your car and win the Saturday gravel rally!

Between wrenching sessions, life goes on. Eat, sleep and heat the sauna. Do odd jobs for the villagers: newspaper rounds, hay bales with the tractor, van deliveries, and driving your drunk neighbour Pertti home. Fish from the dock, chop firewood, buy beer and spare parts in the village, and keep an eye out for the police when you drive without plates.

A clear step-by-step guide with yellow arrows shows you where to go and what to do next, so you never get lost. Works on desktop and mobile with touch controls.

Features:
- Build a car from about 100 real parts: fit, bolt down and tune everything yourself
- Engine hoist, battery charger, jack stands, fluids and a working ignition
- Drive a van, a moped, a tractor and your own car on a big open map
- Jobs, shops, a pub, a sauna by the lake, fishing and a weekly rally
- Guided tutorial with arrows in the world
- Day and night, weather, police, crashes and plenty of funny situations

## Controls (desktop)
On foot:
- WASD – move, Shift – run, Space – jump, C – crouch
- Mouse – look around
- Left mouse button – pick up / drop
- Right mouse button – throw
- E – use, talk, fit the part you are holding, eat / drink, open doors and the hood
- F (or 1 / 2 / 3) – hands / wrench / screwdriver
- Mouse wheel (with the wrench) – tighten / loosen bolts
- Mouse wheel (with the screwdriver) – tune the carburettor and ignition
- Tab – car checklist (what is still missing), M – map
- P – pee, T – check the time, H – hide hints, Esc – pause

Driving:
- W / S – gas / brake, A / D – steer, Space – handbrake
- Shift or X – gear up, Q or Z – gear down
- I (hold) – start the engine, K – choke, L – lights, B – seatbelt
- H – horn, R – radio, N – next station, V – camera, E – get out
- Tractor: R / F – raise / lower the bale fork, G – drop bale

## Controls (mobile)
- Drag on the left side of the screen – walk / steer
- Drag on the right side – look around
- USE – use, talk, fit parts, eat and drink
- GRAB / THROW – pick up, drop and throw items
- Tool button – hands / wrench / screwdriver, TIGHTEN / LOOSEN – bolts
- In a vehicle: GAS / BRAKE pedals, START (hold), gear buttons, HAND BRAKE, EXIT
- Top bar: menu, MAP, CAR LIST
